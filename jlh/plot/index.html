Plot
.

This movie is set during the warring states period in china.

The movie opens with a carraige, surrounded by mounted soldiers. They are headed to the Qin palace.
Inside the carraige is a nameless soldier, played by Jet Li. He will be refered to as 'Nameless'

Accompanying Nameless is an old man. He is similarly not given a name. He will be refered to as 'Old Servant'

Nameless, and the Old Servant are greeted at the Qin palace.

It is revealed that Nameless has killed three assassins who were aiming to kill the king.

Sky, Flying Snow, and Broken Sword

Nameless is invited inside the palace, and changed into now clothes.

He is then informed to never step within 100 paces of the king, or he shall be executed

.
.

The king welcomes nameless inside the palace's main hall. It is very spacious, due to the assassins.

After Nameless bows to him, the king tells Nameless of the assassins

The king then opens a box containing a spear tip.

This is the spear tip that belongs to Sky, the silver spear. In reward, Nameless is given gold, control over many households, allowed to move within 20 paces of the king.

The king beckons Nameless to tell him the story of how he defeated Sky.

Nameless says that he encountered Sky in a music and chess house. The local authorities attempt to arrest Sky, and fail.

Nameless then recounts his fight with Sky. Nameless and Sky battle across the music house, flying back and forth.

Nameless eventually defeats Sky after an intense mental battle.

.
.

The king compliments Nameless' skill, and invites him to move within 10 paces for killing Flying Snow and Broken Sword.

Nameless then tells the story. He travelled to a calligraphy school where Broken Sword and Flying Snow resided. He commisioned a new way to make the symbol "Sword" from Broken Sword

While Broken Sword is working, the city gets besieged by the king's army. Nameless helps fend off arrows, and meets Flying Snow

Nameless recieves the calligraphy from Broken Sword, and spends the rest of the day analyzing it, to find out about Broken Sword's swordmanship.

That night, he goes to the school library. Across from him is Flying Snow on one side, and Broken Sword and Moon on the other. Nameless tells them about Sky's death, and makes a remark about Flying Snow and Sky's relationship.

This remark angers Broken Sword. It is shown that a few days later, he takes advantage of his servant, to make Flying Snow jealous.

.
.

Flying Snow spies on Broken Sword, and is angered. She takes her sword, and thrusts it through a wall, killing Broken Sword.

The next day, the servant finds Flying Snow, and seeks revenge for her master. The servant is defeated.

Regretting her actions, Flying Snow goes to a Qin army camp to duel Nameless, who she blames for Broken Sword's death.

Because of her mental state, Nameless is able to easily overpower the angry and uncoordinated Flying Snow.

The king questions this turn of events, saying that the Broken Sword and Flying Snow that he fought 3 years ago were very mentally strong, and would not be dealt with so easily.

The king goes on to say that Nameless is probably an assassin who is also after him, and that Snow had never heard of Sky.

The king says that Nameles probably has a technique that would allow him to kill the king from 10 paces, which he also used to kill Sky.

.
.

The king then tells Nameless what he thinks happened.

After fending off the Qin siege, Nameless goes to the library. He tells Broken Sword and Flying Snow his plan. He demonstrates it by tossing a cup of tea, and cutting every scroll within 10 paces before the cup of tea falls down.

Now thoroughly convinced, the lovers agree to Nameless' plan.

Before they can meet up with him at the Qin camp, Broken Sword is given a non-fatal wound by Flying Snow. Flying Snow goes to the Qin camp alone, and is willingly defeated.

Flying Snow's body is recovered by Broken Sword, and is taken to a lake.

.
.

Nameless meets Broken Sword at the lake, and they have a ceremonial battle. This battle happens because Broken Sword has sworn to battle anyone who harms Flying Snow.

After this battle, Nameless heads on his way to the Qin palace, to kill the king

Nameless tells the king that he indeed plans to assassinate him. Nameless also tells the king what actually happened.

After the siege, Nameless goes to the library. He tells the lovers his plan, demonstrates the accuracy of his blade, and tells the lovers that sky is still alive. Everyone but Broken Sword agrees. Broken Sword's servant has a fight with Flying Snow, which is broken up by Nameless

Despite Broken Sword not approving of the plan, Flying Snow goes and duels Nameless in the Qin camp.

.
.

After winning the duel with Flying Snow, Nameless is about to leave for the palace. He is accompanied by an older servant, who will report to Flying Snow if the king was actually assassinated.

Broken Sword and Moon meet Nameless. Nameless asks Broken Sword why he refuses to kill the king

The movie flashes back to 10 years ago, when Broken Sword and Flying Snow met. They are shown to be training together, and it is explained that they reside at the calligraphy school to aid their swordsmanship.

We then see them storm the imperial Qin palace, and have a fight with the king himself. Broken Sword refuses to kill him, and retreats.

The inner palace is decorated in green banners, which the king cuts down. This is why the inner palace is so open.

.
.

Broken Sword replies to Nameless by drawing the symbol for tianxia in the sand. He says that there has been continual warfare, which is hurting the people. Only the Qin king has the power to bring peace, despite the cruel means.

This does not seem to pursuade Nameless, and he heads off to the Qin palace.

The king is amazed by this turn of events, and is thoroughly impresse by Broken Sword.

The king asks how Nameless plans to kill him without a sword. Nameless answers that he plans to take the king's sword. The king tosses it too him.

Eunuchs rush the inner palace, but are very careful.

.
.

Soldiers rush in after them, as Nameless prepares to kill the king.

The king comes to a realization about the calligraphy. He says that it is not the essence of Broken Sword's swordsmanship, but it is his highest ideal.

The final stage is revealed to be where only peace exists.

Nameless leaps forward, and instead of stabbing the king, he hits him with the hilt of the sword. He urges the king to not forget the warrior's ideals

.
.

Nameless walks back down the palace steps, surrounded by Guards.

The old servant returns to Flying Snow bearing the news that the assassination has failed.

Broken Sword and Flying Snow duel.

Broken Sword gives up, and lets himself be mortally wounded.

Sometime later, Flying Snow commits suicide, finally seeing what Broken Sword had been saying.

.
.

Nameless is reluctantly executed by the king, to uphold the law, and given a hero's funeral.
