Course Connections

Qin Shi Huangdi's Roads

Hero takes place during the warring states period in China. One key to the Qin state's victory was its robust military. The Qin army's roads were wide, allowing them to travel quite quickly. The Nameless hero mentions how the king's army was very fierce, because of their ability to besiege other cities with skilled, and numerous, archers. These archers, and their artillery, were able to be transported quickly due to Qin Shi Huangdi's roads. We discussed these roads, along with Roman roads during the module 4 lecture.

These roads were also really large. For main army roads, they could get up to 60 meters wide. One 13 mile stretch of road found in China by the Yulin Institute for Cultural Heritage Protection in Shaanxi province averaged 40 meters in width, and was 60 meters wide at its peak. This section of road was announced to be found on December 9th, 2025. The road is about as wide as a modern 4-lane highway today. According to a Futura Sciences article, this road was constructed from 212 BCE to 207 BCE.

Read more about it at https://www.futura-sciences.com/en/2200-year-old-imperial-road-in-china-stuns-modern-engineers-with-its-design_33258/.

Work Processes and Technological Systems in Hero

A work process is all of the people that are needed to get a job done, and a technological system is all of the things that the work system requires. We learned about this in the first module two lecture. In Hero, we are introduced to a multitude of Work Processes and Technological Systems. One example is the royal Qin army. They employ a ton of archers, along with people to carry arrows, horses for transport, and additional people to help set up more powerful weaponry. The Technological System here are the arrows, which are shot from the bows, which are made from cutting down trees with an ax.

Another example is the calligraphy school Broken Sword and Flying Snow reside at. To create calligraphy, you first need a calligrapher. Someone has to teach the calligrapher, and someone has to teach them. The calligrapher also may need someone to commission calligraphy from them. Furthermore, someone needs to make the paper and ink for calligraphy. Hero takes place during the unification period of China, and this work could not be mechanized. People would have to make the paper, and the ink, and bring it to the calligraphy school. For the Technology System, which is all the things you need to do a job, calligraphy would need a pen or brush, paper, and ink for the pen or brush. In Hero, Broken Sword is shown using sand to practice calligraphy, which would add sand, and a rod (for calligraphy) to the list.

Craftspeople and Artisans in Hero

Hero is an epic wuxia or martial arts film. Martial artists are not farmers, and I would consider them to be trained workers. Even though martial artists do not produce any goods, they are still trained for years, with apprenticeships. Nameless trained for 10 years to perfect his “Death within 10 paces” technique, despite never using it as he originally intended. Broken Sword and Flying Snow practiced calligraphy for 10 years, to train their swordsmanship. They eventually became masters, and had their own students, like artisans would.

Speaking of calligraphy, the calligraphy school Broken Sword and Flying Snow live at has resemblances to some industries that we discussed during module 2. The first of which is the Iron industry, and the company town. The calligraphy school is located in a city, but many members of it are shown to have a residence within the school itself. Specifically Broken Sword and Flying Snow. While not explicitly shown having a residence, all of the calligraphy students remain behind in the city while everyone else leaves. There are also glimpses of rooms that are shown, which mirror the quarters that Broken Sword and Flying Snow have. The calligraphy school might be too small to be considered a “town”, but there are still similarities.

Another similarity is that there is one individual who has control over the entire company town or building. This is the master calligrapher, who is a very old man. He decides to take a stand and still practice calligraphy during the siege on the town. By doing this he convinces the rest of the students to stay, which leads to some death.
